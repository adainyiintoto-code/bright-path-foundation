What your sponsorship covers

Tuition, supplies, and a mentor who checks in.

Three programs, funded directly by sponsors, tracked per girl — not pooled into a general fund.

Program 01

Tuition & Supplies

Full school fees, books, and a uniform for one girl, one term — handed over directly, not routed through a general pool. Every sponsor gets a note confirming exactly what was covered.

Program 02

Mentorship

Most school-supplies charities stop at the backpack. We don't. Every sponsored girl is paired with a mentor who checks in monthly — schoolwork, confidence, whatever she needs that term. It's the part that keeps girls in school past the age where many are pulled out.
