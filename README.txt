=====================================

(Programs & Impact), involved.html (Get Involved / sponsor form),
contact.html, thanks.html (post-submission confirmation).

BEFORE DEPLOYING:
1. Deploy to Vercel via GitHub (create a repo, upload these files
   including the images/ folder, connect to Vercel).
2. Once live, note your real URL.
3. In involved.html, replace both "REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_DOMAIN" hidden
   fields (_next and _url) with your exact live domain.
4. Submit the form once on the LIVE site to trigger FormSubmit's
   one-time activation email to chukwumeziefavour88@gmail.com —
   check spam, click the confirmation link, then test again.

  into view), and a hover transition on the donate button — all
  respecting prefers-reduced-motion.
- Signature element: the "sponsorship ledger" on the homepage, an
  itemized receipt-style breakdown of what a sponsorship funds.
- SEO basics: unique title + meta description per page.
